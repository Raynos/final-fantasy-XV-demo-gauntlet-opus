## The wind

The night opened on the top item in the rescue ledger: the same shot came back
different alone versus sixth in a batch, 1.836 mean/255 against a 0.302 floor.
Every document on record said the cause was vegetation tile streaming.

It was not. Pinning vegetation streaming moved the number by **0.009**.

Streaming was genuinely nondeterministic — grass, scrub and trees each built
tiles against a `performance.now() + budgetMs` wall-clock deadline, so residency
depended on machine speed and on what the previous camera had cached — and that
is now fixed with a `converge()` called from `Game.settle`. But it was not what
anyone was seeing.

The carrier was the **wind**. `Weather.resetClock` set only `_snap`, a flag that
skips the lerp toward the target preset. The gust envelope `_gust` is integrated
forever and drives `windStrength` through three sines; `windDir` drifts
permanently; neither is part of `target`, so no preset change and no clock reset
had ever touched them. Probed directly: `windStrength` **0.840** on a page's
first shot, **0.944** after one other shot.

Grass, scrub, twigs and hair all sway off that number, which is why the
difference sat on thin silhouettes and read, to everyone who looked at the mean,
as noise. **1.836 → 0.340.** `imgdiff` at 1.5/255 means something now.

The lesson went into `LANDMINES.md` in the form that generalises: *when a visual
difference has no obvious carrier, print the state, do not stare at the frame.*

## Four lanes, then six

Agents ran in worktrees on disjoint directories, capped at four concurrent.
Boot, instruments, content, character art; then graphics-ceiling, atmosphere,
modeling, inventory as the first wave landed.

**Boot: 13.66 s → 6.88 s cold.** The plan's "single highest-leverage idea, and
it is untested" — caching generated textures the way the terrain field was
already cached — worked, and was held to byte-equality against `?nobake=1`
rather than to a plausible screenshot. Two of the plan's premises were wrong on
inspection: `Dungeons` was always lazy, and `Props.landmarks` was not the
landmarks at all but `PropMaterials` memoisation landing on the first caller.
`compileAsync` was closed as a **measured negative**: 3% slower, because it
resolves 134 programs against `compile`'s 112. What would move it is fewer
programs.

**Wave 1 closed.** A perf ruler that measures its own noise floor and voids the
run rather than printing a number it cannot stand behind — it promptly voided
`perf` at a floor IQR of 27% of a frame, which is the instrument working.
`seatHeightAt` at 0.000 m residual from 60 m to 3.4 km. Ablation dials, and the
rule *ablate before re-tinting* written into `BRIEF.md`.

**Nothing in the game was pressable.** `CombatSystem` bound `KeyE` to
`warpToPoint()`, and Combat runs as system 11 while Interaction runs as 21 — so
every press warped Noctis twelve metres before `Interactables.update` ran. Every
shop, hunt board, caravan, fuel pump, Regalia and NPC advertised a prompt none
of them could honour. Separately, all three RPG coordinate tables were hand-typed
against a 3 km world that later grew to 8 km, so camping answered `no-haven`
everywhere and **no `reach` objective in the game could ever complete**.

## The grade

The most valuable thing built tonight is probably the least visible: a blind A/B
judge with sealed keys, and 659 lines of *pixel-sampled* FFXV reference with the
53 plates it was drawn from.

The game scores **3/10**: six identified, zero fooled. The 4.5/10 everyone had
been flying on was stale and unfalsifiable.

And the instrument immediately paid for itself. An attempt to close the measured
`R−B +15.6` warmth gap in the colour grade moved the number by **1.4 levels** and
was reverted as a measured negative — because the grade was not the cause. Our
bright quartile is a near-neutral grey cloud blanket where FFXV's is blue sky.
Two of the judge's five ranked defects collapse into one cause in `Clouds.ts`.

## What the harness was still lying about

Twice tonight `npm run check` reported that the terrain had regressed. Both
times `heightcheck` and `driftcheck` passed standalone at 0.000 m. They were
never running: `serve()` resolved on a log line with a fifteen-second timer that
resolved **successfully** if the line never came, and a cold bake holds vite for
41 seconds before it listens. Two lanes each spent an investigation on it.

It polls the socket now, the aux port is scanned rather than assumed, and a
gate that measured nothing renders as **VOID** rather than FAIL — because a void
run that reads as a regression sends the next person after a number nobody took.

## The shape of the night

Almost every hour produced the same shape: a confident sentence in a document,
an instrument that disagreed with it, and a cause somewhere else entirely. The
plans were good and their diagnoses were frequently wrong — which is an argument
for writing plans *and* for never trusting one past the first measurement.
